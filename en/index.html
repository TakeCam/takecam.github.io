Support

Questions, problems or suggestions: write to takecamsupport@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, your iOS version and what happened.

The session stopped on its own

TakeCam only records while the app is open and the iPhone is unlocked. Locking the iPhone or switching apps ends the session; the video being recorded is saved. A phone call or another camera interruption only pauses it: the session continues when the camera comes back, and ends if it stays away for more than 10 minutes. To avoid this, use Guided Access (Settings › Accessibility) to keep the iPhone in the app, and turn on Do Not Disturb or Airplane Mode with Wi-Fi on. The app also ends the session if the iPhone gets too hot.

Where the videos are

You choose in Settings › Where to save (and on first launch): In Photos, in a session album ("TakeCam" followed by the date) and, all together, in the "TakeCam – All Recordings" album; Only in the app, in TakeCam’s own folder (Files › TakeCam › gravacoes), without showing in Photos; or SSD folder, in the folder you chose on an external SSD, with Photos as backup. Videos from an interrupted session that the app managed to recover are in Files › TakeCam › recuperados.

The Recordings screen

From the Recordings button (Prepare screen) you see the sessions from any of those places: watch the videos, name sessions, join sessions into one, make a single video out of all the videos of a session, delete what you don’t want and, for sessions kept only in the app, choose which videos to send to Photos. Sent videos get a photo badge and, if you delete them in the app, the Photos copy is deleted too (iOS asks you to confirm).

The Mac viewer does not open

The Mac and the iPhone must be on the same Wi-Fi network and TakeCam must be open. Use "Open on Mac" (AirDrop) or "Copy address" on the main screen: the address already includes the 6-digit code. The first time, the iPhone asks whether TakeCam may access the local network; answer yes (you can change it in Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network).

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026.

In short: TakeCam does not collect any data about you. There is no account, sign-up, advertising, analytics or tracking, and the app sends nothing to any server, neither the developer’s nor anyone else’s.

Camera and microphone

Used only to record the videos of the sessions you start, always with sound. No image or sound is analyzed or sent off the device; the app only measures the volume of each recorded video, on the iPhone itself, to warn you if one came out silent.

Photos and files

Videos are saved on your iPhone: in an album in the Photos app, in TakeCam’s own folder or in an SSD folder you choose. Sending a video from the app to Photos makes a copy; the app keeps, in the session folder, which Photos item corresponds to each sent video, only to mark it and delete the copy along with it if you ask. The names you give to sessions are kept in a file on the iPhone itself. The app also keeps, on the iPhone itself, a log of each session (settings used, the time of each video, warnings, battery, temperature and free space) to show the summary and help understand problems. These files appear in the Files app and you can delete them at any time. If iCloud Photos is on, Apple syncs the videos according to your iCloud settings; TakeCam takes no part in that.

Local network (Mac viewer)

While the app is open, the iPhone offers a viewer on your local Wi-Fi network, so you can see the image and control the session from another device’s browser. It only works inside the local network, never over the internet, and requires a 6-digit code shown in the app. The connection uses unencrypted HTTP: on networks you do not control (hotel, café, office), someone with technical access to the network could observe the traffic. The viewer is optional.

People in the videos

You are responsible for what you record. Tell the people who appear in the videos and obtain any consent your local law requires.

Children

TakeCam does not collect data from anyone, including children.
